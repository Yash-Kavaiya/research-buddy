# Indian research funding schemes: orientation

Use this file to find the right scheme to look up. It is a map, and it goes out of date. Eligibility, age limits, amounts, fellowship rates, and dates must be taken from the funder's current call document every time.

Last reviewed: October 2026. In that review only the ANRF programme list was read from the official portal. The one-line descriptions, and the DST, UGC, and other-funder sections, are orientation written from general knowledge and secondary sources, so verify them before relying on them.

## ANRF (Anusandhan National Research Foundation), formerly SERB

ANRF was established under the ANRF Act, 2023, and took over the functions of the Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB). Applications go through the ANRF online portal (anrfonline.in), which lists current ANRF programmes separately from "erstwhile SERB schemes". Many researchers still say "SERB" for these grants; check which name and scheme is currently open.

Programmes listed as current on the ANRF portal (as read in October 2026):

| Programme | Who it is for (confirm on the call) |
|---|---|
| Prime Minister Early Career Research Grant (PM ECRG) | Early-career faculty and scientists starting an independent group. Took over the role of SERB's Start-up Research Grant (SRG). Holders of earlier SERB or ANRF project grants are generally not eligible. |
| Advanced Research Grant (ARG) | Investigator-initiated research projects. It occupies the place SERB's Core Research Grant (CRG) held; the portal also still lists CRG, so confirm on the call how the two relate. |
| ARG MATRICS | Mathematical and theoretical sciences; carries the name of SERB's MATRICS scheme. |
| Inclusivity Research Grant (IRG) | Researchers from groups the scheme defines as under-represented. |
| National Post Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF) | Recent PhD holders, working under a mentor at an Indian institution. |
| Ramanujan Fellowship | Researchers returning to India from abroad. |
| J. C. Bose Grant (JBG) | Senior scientists with an outstanding record. |
| National Science Chair (NSC) | Eminent senior and superannuated scientists. |
| Partnerships for Accelerated Innovation and Research (PAIR) | Hub-and-spoke partnerships pairing established institutions with universities building research capacity. |
| Prime Minister Professorship | Senior researchers hosted at state universities. |
| International Travel Support (ITS) | Travel to present a paper at an international conference. |
| Seminar/Symposia (SSY) | Support for organising scientific events. |
| Mission-mode and centre programmes | For example MAHA missions (EV, MedTech and others), Convergence Research Centres of Excellence, Impact Centres of Excellence, AI for Science and Engineering, Translational Research and Innovation. Called by theme. |

Erstwhile SERB scheme names you will still meet in CVs, older guidance, and ongoing projects: Core Research Grant (CRG), Start-up Research Grant (SRG), Early Career Research Award (ECR), MATRICS, SUPRA, IRHPA, TARE (Teachers Associateship for Research Excellence), EMEQ, SERB-POWER (women researchers), SERB-SURE (state universities), VAJRA, OVDF, SIRE, SERB-STAR.

A 2026 application calendar was published by ANRF covering JC Bose Grant, N-PDF, Ramanujan Fellowship, and other schemes; look for the current year's calendar on the portal.

## DST (Department of Science and Technology)

Portal: dst.gov.in and onlinedst.gov.in. Calls are issued by programme division.

| Scheme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| INSPIRE Fellowship | Doctoral fellowship for high-ranking postgraduates in basic and applied sciences. |
| INSPIRE Faculty Fellowship | Five-year independent position for young PhD holders; age-limited. |
| WISE-KIRAN (Women in Science and Engineering) | Fellowships and project support for women scientists, including WISE-PhD, WISE-PDF, and schemes for women returning after a career break. |
| FIST | Infrastructure for university departments and institutions (department-level, not individual). |
| PURSE | Research capacity support for universities, selected on publication performance. |
| SATHI, SAIF | Shared analytical instrumentation facilities. |
| Technology Development Programme and related calls | Applied and translational projects; often thematic. |
| National missions (for example quantum, interdisciplinary cyber-physical systems, supercomputing) | Thematic calls through mission hubs. |
| International bilateral and multilateral calls | Joint projects with partner countries; announced per country. |

## UGC (University Grants Commission)

Portal: ugc.gov.in; fellowships are administered through the UGC fellowship portal, and UGC-NET is conducted by NTA.

| Scheme | Purpose |
|---|---|
| UGC-NET Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), then SRF | Doctoral fellowship awarded through the NET examination. Monthly rates are revised periodically by government order; quote the current order, as web sources disagree on the amount. |
| Savitribai Jyotirao Phule Fellowship for Single Girl Child | Doctoral fellowship. |
| National Fellowships for SC, ST, OBC, and for persons with disabilities | Doctoral fellowships now largely administered by the respective ministries; check the current administering body. |
| Dr. D. S. Kothari Research Grant / post-doctoral schemes | Support for newly recruited faculty and post-doctoral researchers; scheme names were restructured in recent years. |
| Research Grant for In-Service Faculty Members; Fellowship for Superannuated Faculty | Faculty research support schemes introduced in the restructuring. |
| Dr. S. Radhakrishnan Post-Doctoral Fellowship | Humanities, social sciences, and languages. |

UGC's older Major and Minor Research Project schemes are widely reported as discontinued; do not plan around them without finding a current call.

The table rows above other than UGC-NET JRF were written from general knowledge of the schemes and were not re-verified against UGC pages in the October 2026 review. Confirm each on ugc.gov.in.

## Other national funders

| Funder | Typical support |
|---|---|
| DBT (Department of Biotechnology) | Life-science projects; DBT-JRF; Ramalingaswami Re-entry Fellowship; BIRAC for translational and industry-linked work. |
| CSIR | CSIR-NET JRF and SRF; Extra-Mural Research (EMR) projects; Research Associateships. |
| ICMR | Biomedical and public-health projects (extramural ad hoc, task force); ICMR JRF and SRF. Requires ethics clearance per ICMR guidelines. |
| ICSSR | Social-science research projects (major, minor), doctoral and post-doctoral fellowships. |
| ICAR | Agricultural research. |
| MeitY | Electronics and IT; Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme. |
| DRDO, ISRO (RESPOND), DAE-BRNS | Sponsored research aligned with each agency's needs; proposals usually need a contact scientist within the agency. |
| AICTE | Schemes for technical institutions (research promotion, MODROBS and similar). |
| Ministry of Education | PMRF (Prime Minister's Research Fellowship) for doctoral study at designated institutions; SPARC and GIAN for international collaboration. |
| State councils of science and technology | Smaller grants, often good for a first project. |

## Things that recur across Indian proposals

- Applications are submitted online by the Principal Investigator and need an endorsement certificate from the head of the host institution on the funder's template.
- Budgets use fixed heads: recurring (manpower, consumables, travel, contingency, other costs) and non-recurring (equipment), plus institutional overheads at the rate the scheme permits. Manpower emoluments follow the government's current office memorandum on research fellowships (JRF, SRF, RA rates and HRA); cite the memorandum in force.
- Equipment needs quotations and a justification; procurement follows GFR and, usually, the GeM portal.
- Most schemes restrict how many projects a PI may hold or apply for at once, and bar simultaneous submission of the same proposal to another agency.
- Required uploads commonly include the PI's biodata in the funder's format, proof of age and employment, a plagiarism undertaking, and, where relevant, ethics, biosafety, or animal-ethics clearance.
- Calls are announced on the funder's site and summarised by institutional research offices, which often publish monthly lists of active calls; the applicant's own research office is the fastest way to a current list.

## How to use this file

1. Shortlist two or three schemes by career stage and field.
2. Open each scheme's current call or guidelines page and extract eligibility, limits, format, and dates into the proposal's compliance checklist.
3. Record the URL and the date read. Treat anything in this file that conflicts with the call as outdated.
